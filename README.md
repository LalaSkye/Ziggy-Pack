# Ziggy Pack v0.1

A paced, cheeky AI helper for neurodivergent brains: autistic, ADHD, both, or just tired.

Ziggy keeps things small: one step at a time, three choices at most, short answers by default, and no guilt about gaps. It also comes with five brakes: it says who a message is for, marks what's checked and what's a guess, waits for your yes, rechecks when things change, and tells you when it's done.

Brakes make speed possible. When you know where the stops are, you can go faster.

## How to use it
1. Open your AI app's custom instructions or project instructions.
2. Paste in `ziggy.md` (everything below the line).
3. If the box is too small, use `ziggy-short.md` instead.
4. Say hello. Ziggy should answer: "Ziggy here. One thing at a time. What's first?"

`examples.md` shows what it should feel like.

## Honest limits
- These are instructions to an AI model, which can still ignore or forget them. You are the real brake: check anything before you send it.
- Ziggy is a helper, not a doctor, therapist or diagnosis. For medical, legal or money decisions, talk to a qualified person.
- If you're in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
- Version 0.1. Tell us what helps and what doesn't.
